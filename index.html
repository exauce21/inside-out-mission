Welcome

We’re so glad you’re here. Whether you’re searching for something more, coming back to faith, or walking closely with God you are welcome. At Inside Out, we believe that Jesus transforms hearts, and from that transformation flows a new life, a new hope, and a deeper love.

About Inside Out

“Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation…” 2 Corinthians 5:17

Inside Out is a Christ-centred outreach founded on the belief that true transformation begins in the heart and flows outward into every part of life. We exist to create a space where people can:

  • Encounter the love of Jesus
  • Receive prayer
  • Engage with God’s Word
  • Begin a journey of spiritual renewal

We believe lives are made new through Jesus not by pressure or perfection, but by the grace and power of the gospel. Our prayer is to see:

  • Hearts changed
  • Hope restored
  • People walking in the freedom only Christ can give

Our mission is to help individuals encounter the life-changing love of Jesus. We believe that a renewed heart leads to a renewed life, from the inside out.

We gather in Clerkenwell, and across Islington, London, to share God’s Word, pray with people, and hand out free Bibles with our hope kits. But our vision is greater than one location we long to see hearts transformed all over the world by the love and power of Jesus.

Why We Do It

Because Jesus transforms lives. We’ve seen it in our own stories and we believe it can happen in yours. The Bible says, “If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation” (2 Corinthians 5:17). No matter your past or your pain, God is ready to renew your heart from the inside out.

Start With a Simple Prayer

You don’t need fancy words. You just need to be honest with God. Try something like this:

God, I’m not even sure what I believe, but I want to know You. If You are real, help me see You. Help me feel Your love and peace. Amen.

or

Jesus, I’m tired. I’ve tried doing life on my own. Please give me a new heart. Fill me with hope. Teach me how to follow You. Amen.

For more prayers, drop in during a session and receive a physical copy of our prayer booklet included in our Hope Kit.

Our Calling

Inside Out is led by volunteers who’ve encountered the love of Jesus and want to share it. We are guided by:

  • The Great Commission “Go and make disciples…” (Matthew 28:19)
  • A deep desire to see lives changed through prayer and God’s Word
  • The call to proclaim the Gospel “Go into all the world and proclaim the gospel to the whole creation.” (Mark 16:15)

We serve as vessels for God’s work, transformed from the inside out.
